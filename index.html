How an engagement works

From brief to documents in days

Brief

Search

Priora runs the patent, FDA and regulation searches and links every finding to its record.

Review

We walk your team through the flags and the landscape, and agree what to dig into.

Deliver

You receive the report, trace matrix and roadmap, ready for your patent counsel and regulatory lead.

Your invention stays private

Confidential design details are processed on private infrastructure and are never sent to public AI services.

Every finding cites its source

Patent numbers, 510(k) numbers and regulation sections sit beside each finding, so anyone can check it.

Flags, not legal opinions
