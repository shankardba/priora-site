A research engine for medical technology

Know what came before you build.

Priora connects the public record of medical technology (FDA clearances, recalls, 26 million adverse-event reports, patent classes and the research literature) and researches the open web with AI models that run on our own hardware. These case studies show how it answers a technical question, start to finish.

How a case is researched

Open data, a custom knowledge base, and open-source AI on one private server.

Every part runs on open-source software on a server the client controls. Questions, drafts and findings never pass through a cloud AI service. Click any box for an explanation, or follow a question through the five steps.

  • Open-source throughoutSearch engine, document readers, language model, graph database: no licence fees and no vendor lock-in.
  • The data stays on siteThe server holds its own copy of each open data set. Confidential questions never leave it.
  • Every claim is checkedA finding stands only if its exact words and numbers are in the source. An expert approves each report.
